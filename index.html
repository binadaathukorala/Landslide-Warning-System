Help Us Save Lives

Your donation helps us deploy more early warning nodes in vulnerable communities and continue improving the system for greater accuracy and coverage.

Every LKR 6,600 Saves a Community

Each monitoring node costs approximately LKR 6,600 - several orders of magnitude cheaper than commercial stations that cost USD 5,000-50,000 per point. Your contribution directly funds:

  • Hardware components for new deployment nodes
  • Weatherproof enclosures and solar panels
  • Field testing and calibration in real conditions
  • Community training and app distribution
  • Research into improving detection accuracy
